🎯 Project Objective & Unit 2 Motion Graph Mastery

In this laboratory project, you act as a sports analytics biomechanist analyzing the two-dimensional projectile motion of a sports ball. In addition to analyzing your video, this project serves as a comprehensive mastery application of Unit 2: Constant Acceleration & Graphing Motion (Test Problems 1, 3, and 4).

How Your Projectile's Motion Directly Mirrors Unit 2 Exam Problem 3:
  • Vertical Motion (\(y, v_y, a_y\)) = Exam Problem 3 Turnaround (\(t = 2\text{–}6\text{ s}\)): The ball starts at initial height \(y_0 \neq 0\), moves upward in the \(+y\) direction while slowing down (\(v_y > 0, a_y < 0\)), stops vertically for an instant at the apex (\(v_y = 0\) where \(y(t)\) tangent slope is zero), and moves downward in the \(-y\) direction while speeding up (\(v_y < 0, a_y < 0\)).
  • Horizontal Motion (\(x, v_x, a_x\)) = Exam Problem 3 Constant Velocity (\(t = 0\text{–}2\text{ s}\)): Linear position \(x(t)\), constant positive horizontal velocity \(v_x(t) = v_{0x}\), and zero horizontal acceleration \(a_x(t) = 0\text{ m/s}^2\).
Companion Graphing Simulations for Practice:
  • Graph Sprint — Practice translating between \(x\)-\(t\), \(v\)-\(t\), and \(a\)-\(t\) graphs (Exam Problem 1 & 3a).
  • Kinematics Stagecrafter — Practice signed \(v\)-\(t\) triangle/rectangle areas (\(\Delta x = \sum\text{Area}\)) and \(x_f = x_0 + \Delta x\) (Exam Problem 3c & 3d).
  • Uniform Acceleration Ramp Lab — Practice turnaround motion (\(v = 0\) at apex while \(a < 0\)) and tangent slopes (Exam Problem 3e–g & Problem 4).
  • Coco's Motion Lab — Match real-time motion to position and velocity graphs.

📈 Step 3: Two-Point Tangent Slopes (Exam Problem 4) & Graph Interpretation (Exam Problem 3)

1. Two-Point Tangent Slope Rule (Exam Problem 4 Format):

In algebra-based physics, instantaneous velocity at any instant \(t\) is the slope of the tangent line touching the position-time curve: \[v_y = \text{slope of tangent} = \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta t} = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{t_2 - t_1}\] To earn full credit (just like Exam Problem 4):

  1. Align the tangent line at time \(t\) using the Tangent Slider.
  2. Read two widely separated points \(P_1(t_1, y_1)\) and \(P_2(t_2, y_2)\) lying ON the drawn tangent line (do NOT pick raw data points on the curve!).
  3. Substitute into \(v_y = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{t_2 - t_1}\) with units (\(\text{m/s}\)) and click Verify \(\Delta y / \Delta t\) & Record. Complete at least 3 verified two-point calculations (ascent, near apex, descent) and 10 total tangent samples.
2. Physical Motion Interval Analysis (Exam Problem 3e, 3f, 3g):
  • Stopping (\(v_y = 0\)): The projectile stops vertically for a single instant at \(t = t_{\text{apex}}\) where \(v_y(t)\) crosses the zero axis and \(y(t)\) has a horizontal tangent (slope \(= 0\)). However, it is NOT stopped in 2D space because \(v_x = v_{0x} \neq 0\)!
  • Moving in \(+y\) vs. \(-y\) Direction: Direction of motion is determined by the sign of velocity (\(v_y > 0\) above the zero axis on \(v_y\)-\(t\) / positive slope on \(y\)-\(t\)), NOT the sign of position or acceleration!
  • Sign of Acceleration (\(a_y < 0\)): Vertical acceleration is negative across the entire flight (\(0 < t < t_{\text{final}}\)) because gravity pulls downward continuously—causing the \(y(t)\) parabola to curve downward (concave down) and the \(v_y(t)\) line to have a constant negative slope \(\frac{\Delta v_y}{\Delta t} \approx -9.8\text{ m/s}^2\).

🧮 Step 4: Signed \(v\)-\(t\) Area Method (Exam Problem 3c–d) & Kinematic Derivations

In Step 4, you will verify your projectile's motion using both the Signed \(v\)-\(t\) Area Method (Exam Problem 3b, 3c, 3d) and Fundamental Kinematic Equations (no shortcuts):

  • Acceleration from \(v_y\)-\(t\) Slope (Exam 3b): State \(a_y = \frac{\Delta v_y}{\Delta t} = \frac{v_{y,f} - v_{0y}}{t_f - 0}\) symbolically first, then substitute two points from your \(v_y(t)\) line with units (\(\text{m/s}^2\)).
  • Displacement from Signed \(v_y\)-\(t\) Triangle Areas (Exam 3c): \[\Delta y_{\text{up}} = \text{Area}_{\text{above}} = +\frac{1}{2} t_{\text{apex}} v_{0y}, \qquad \Delta y_{\text{down}} = \text{Area}_{\text{below}} = \frac{1}{2}(t_{\text{hang}} - t_{\text{apex}})v_{fy} < 0\] \[\Delta y_{\text{total}} = \Delta y_{\text{up}} + \Delta y_{\text{down}}\]
  • Position from Initial Position + Signed Area (Exam 3d): Never forget initial height \(y_0\)! \[y_{\max} = y_0 + \Delta y_{\text{up}}, \qquad y_{\text{final}} = y_0 + \Delta y_{\text{total}}\]
  • Fundamental Kinematic Equations: \[v_{fy}^2 = v_{iy}^2 + 2a_y(y_{\max} - y_0), \qquad y_f = y_0 + v_{0y}t + \frac{1}{2}a_y t^2, \qquad \Delta x = v_{0x}t + \frac{1}{2}a_x t^2\]
  • Percent Difference Formula: \[\text{\% Diff} = \frac{\text{Calculated} - \text{Measured}}{\text{Measured}} \times 100\%\]

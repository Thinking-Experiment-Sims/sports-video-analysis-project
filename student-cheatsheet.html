1. Step-by-Step Workflow (Tabs 1 → 3)

Tabs 1 & 2: Calibrate, Track & Predict

  • Open Video & Set FPS: Click 📹 Open Video. Set FPS to your phone's real rate (normal = 30 or 60; iPhone slow-mo = 120 or 240).
  • 1. Set Scale (Do First!): Click 📏 1. Set Scale, drag across your meter stick/known length in the frame, enter meters in 1A, and click Apply Scale.
  • 2. Set Origin: Click 🎯 2. Set Origin and place the crosshairs with \(y = 0\) on the floor and \(x = 0\) directly below launch.
  • 3. Track Ball: Step forward until the ball cleanly leaves the hand/launcher. Select ➕ 3. Track Ball and click the ball's center 10–15 times across rise, apex, and fall. Stop before bounce/catch!
  • 4. Set \(t_{\text{up}}\) (Step 1B): Click Use Peak Frame t (or type the time of the highest tracked frame).
  • Tab 2 (Predict): Choose shapes for \(v_x, v_y, a_x, a_y\), write a 1-sentence reason, and click 🔒 Lock Prediction & Go to Tab 3: Build Graphs →.
    Shot from right to left? Choose Flat negative horizontal line (\(v_x < 0\))!

Tab 3: Build All 6 Motion Graphs (3A → 3D)

  • 3A. Position Fits:
    • Horizontal \(x(t)\) → select Linear (\(x = mt + c\))
    • Vertical \(y(t)\) → select Quadratic (\(At^2+Bt+C\))
  • 3B. Tangent Slopes → Velocity:
    • On \(y(t)\), click Ascent, Apex, and Descent, pressing ➕ Plot This Tangent Slope at least 3 times (5 recommended).
    • Vertical \(v_y(t)\) → select Linear (\(v_y = a_y t + v_{0y}\))
    • Horizontal \(v_x(t)\) → select Constant (\(v_x = m\))
  • 3C. Acceleration Fits:
    • Horizontal \(a_x(t)\) → select Constant Zero (\(a_x = 0\))
    • Vertical \(a_y(t)\) → select Constant (\(a_y = \text{slope of } v_y\))
  • 3D. Review & Notes: Inspect all 6 graphs (Extended: toggle 📐 Shade Signed \(v_y\)–\(t\) Areas), then write how your predictions compared and how \(v\) and \(a\) come from slopes.
2. Best-Fit Equation Decoder Ring (Displayed at the Top of Tab 4)

Horizontal Motion: Best-Fit Line \(x(t)\)

$$x(t) = m\,t + c$$
  • Initial horizontal position: \(x_0 = c\) (m)
  • Initial horizontal velocity: \(v_{0x} = m\) (m/s) — constant!
  • Horizontal acceleration: \(a_x = 0\text{ m/s}^2\) (no air drag model)
  • Direction sign rule: Shot left-to-right \(\Rightarrow m > 0\). Shot right-to-left \(\Rightarrow m < 0\) (keep the negative sign!).

Vertical Motion: Best-Fit Curve \(y(t)\)

$$y(t) = A\,t^2 + B\,t + C$$
  • Initial vertical height: \(y_0 = C\) (m)
  • Initial vertical velocity: \(v_{0y} = B\) (m/s)
  • Vertical acceleration: \(a_y = \mathbf{2A}\) (m/s\(^2\)) — WATCH OUT! From \(y = y_0 + v_{0y}t + \tfrac{1}{2}a_y t^2\), \(A = \tfrac{1}{2}a_y\), so you MUST multiply \(A\) by 2 (keep the minus sign, expect near \(-9.8\text{ m/s}^2\)).

Important Symbols & Reminders

\(t_{\text{hang}}\) (Flight Time): Time from first tracked point (\(t = 0\)) to last tracked point. Do NOT assume \(t_{\text{hang}} = 2t_{\text{up}}\) unless launch and end heights match!
\(t_{\text{up}}\) (Rise Time): Observed time from \(t = 0\) to the highest tracked frame in Step 1B. Precision is \(\pm \Delta t\) between tracked frames.
\(y_{\text{final}}\) (End Height): Height \(y\) of your last tracked frame (usually not zero because tracking stops before ground impact!).

3. Tab 4: Kinematics Calculations 1–7 (Standard Version)
Percent Difference Formula (Calc 1–4 & 7): \(\;\text{\% diff} = \dfrac{|\text{Calculated} - \text{Measured (or Reference)}|}{|\text{Measured (or Reference)}|} \times 100\%\) Work Requirement: Write equation + numbers + units in every Work box!
Calculation How to Calculate (Best-Fit Model Values) What to Measure / Compare With
1. Fit Values Read directly from your Tab 3 equations: \(x_0 = c\), \(v_{0x} = m\), \(a_x = 0\), \(y_0 = C\), \(v_{0y} = B\), and \(a_y = \mathbf{2A}\). Compare \(a_y\) against reference \(-9.8\text{ m/s}^2\) (% diff). Compare \(a_x\) with \(0\text{ m/s}^2\) (no % diff).
2. Hang Time \(t_{\text{hang}}\) Solve \(y_{\text{final}} = y_0 + v_{0y}t + \tfrac{1}{2}a_y t^2 \Rightarrow \left(\tfrac{1}{2}a_y\right)t^2 + v_{0y}t + (y_0 - y_{\text{final}}) = 0\) with the quadratic formula:
\(t = \dfrac{-v_{0y} \pm \sqrt{v_{0y}^2 - 4\left(\tfrac{1}{2}a_y\right)(y_0 - y_{\text{final}})}}{a_y}\). Keep the larger positive descent root; state which root you rejected and why.		 Measured: Last tracked time minus first tracked time (shown as \(t_{\text{hang}}\) in the pinned Tab 4 bar).
3. Range \(\Delta x\) Use \(\Delta x = v_{0x}\,t_{\text{calc}} + \tfrac{1}{2}a_x t_{\text{calc}}^2 = v_{0x}\,t_{\text{calc}}\) (since \(a_x = 0\), plug in your calculated hang time from #2). Measured: \(x_{\text{final}} - x_{\text{initial}}\) from Tab 1C table (or \(\Delta x\) in Tab 4 reference bar).
4. Max Height \(y_{\max}\) At apex, \(v_y = 0\): use \(0 = v_{0y}^2 + 2a_y \Delta y_{\text{up}} \Rightarrow \Delta y_{\text{up}} = \dfrac{-v_{0y}^2}{2a_y} = \dfrac{v_{0y}^2}{2|a_y|}\). Then add \(y_0\): \(y_{\max} = y_0 + \Delta y_{\text{up}}\). Measured: Highest \(y\) value in your Tab 1C table (shown as \(y_{\max}\) in Tab 4 reference bar).
5. Launch Speed \(v_0\) Combine \(x\) and \(y\) components: \(v_0 = \sqrt{v_{0x}^2 + v_{0y}^2}\). Magnitude only (always positive, units: m/s).
6. Launch Angle \(\theta\) Make sure calculator is in DEG (degrees) mode! Calculate \(\theta = \tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{|v_{0y}|}{|v_{0x}|}\right)\) above the horizontal (or \(180^\circ - \theta\) from \(+x\) if shot right-to-left). Check: steep lob > \(45^\circ\), low line drive < \(45^\circ\).
7. Consistency Check: Gravity \(g_{\text{meas}}\) From measured rise \(\Delta y_{\text{up}} = y_{\max,\text{meas}} - y_0\) and observed video rise time \(t_{\text{up}}\), use \(\Delta y_{\text{up}} = \tfrac{1}{2}g t_{\text{up}}^2\) to compute:
\(g_{\text{meas}} = \dfrac{2(y_{\max,\text{meas}} - y_0)}{t_{\text{up}}^2}\)		 Compute % diff vs. fitted \(|a_y|\) (from Calc 1) AND % diff vs. \(9.8\text{ m/s}^2\). State in 1 sentence whether your two methods agree.
Extended Mode Quick Note (5a–5f & 5A): In Extended mode, 5a–5b take two-point slopes from the fitted lines; 5c predicts \(v_{0y,\text{pred}} = -(-9.8)\,t_{\text{up}}\) and compares with \(B\); 5d–5e use \(a_{y,\text{ref}} = -9.8\text{ m/s}^2\); and 5A splits signed triangles at \(t_{\text{zero}}\) so \(C + \text{Area}_{\text{up}} + \text{Area}_{\text{down}} \approx y_{\text{fit}}(t_{\text{hang}})\).
4. Tab 5 Key Explanations & Before-You-Ask Troubleshooting

Tab 5B Conceptual Checklist

  • Is the ball at rest at the apex (\(v_y = 0\))? No! Only its vertical velocity is instantaneously zero while switching from up to down. Its horizontal velocity \(v_x\) is still constant and non-zero, so it is moving horizontally in 2D.
  • Why is \(a_y\) negative on BOTH sides of the apex? Gravity pulls downward (\(-y\)) before, at, and after the peak. On the way up (\(v_y > 0\)), negative \(a_y\) opposes motion and slows the ball down. On the way down (\(v_y < 0\)), negative \(a_y\) matches motion and speeds the ball up downward.
  • Limitation + Direction (Required): Name one concrete limit (frame spacing hiding the true apex, motion blur, or scale bar closer/farther than the ball) AND explain whether it made your result too high or too low.
  • Gravity Conclusion: State your \(a_y\) vs. \(-9.8\text{ m/s}^2\) % difference; if >15% off, explain whether wrong FPS or out-of-plane scale caused it.

FAQ: Fixes Before Asking Your Teacher

Why is "✓ Check My Numbers" greyed out / disabled? Type at least a brief equation/substitution into at least one Work box in Tab 4 first. Checks are unlocked once written work is entered!
Red Banner: "Your FPS setting may be 2×/4× off"? Slow-motion clips often export at 30 fps or play at 120/240 fps. Change the FPS box in Tab 1 (try 30, 60, 120, or 240) until \(a_y = 2A\) is near \(-9.8\text{ m/s}^2\).
Points & scale shifted when reloading video + .json? Use the 🎯 Align Overlay to Video sliders (X, Y, Scale) at the top of Tab 1 to visually line points back onto your video—your numbers and calculations stay untouched!
How do I save progress & submit without printing? Click 💾 Save (.json) anytime to back up work. In Tab 6, enter Name & Teacher, click 🖨️ Print / Save Report as PDF, and change Destination to Save as PDF (do not print paper).

Teacher & Department Guide: Physics of Sports Analytics Video Project

A complete instructional guide explaining how this project replaces Logger Pro, enforces algebra-based kinematics derivations without shortcuts, verifies academic integrity, and directly remediates Unit 2: Constant Acceleration & Motion Graphing (Exam Problems 1, 3, and 4).

1. Why We Designed & Upgraded This Project

In our algebra-based Physics course, the Sports Analytics Video Project serves two instructional goals: (1) applying 2D projectile kinematics to real athletic motion recorded by students, and (2) reinforcing and reassessing the exact Unit 2 Motion Graphing skills where students struggled on the Fall Constant Acceleration Exam.

Four Core Pedagogical Rules Built into the Simulation:
  1. Algebra-Based $\Delta$ Notation Only (No Calculus): Every formula and explanation uses $\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta t}$, $\frac{\Delta v_y}{\Delta t}$, and geometric triangle/rectangle area ($\sum \text{Area}$)—never derivatives or integrals.
  2. No Points for Computer-Plotted Graphs: Because the software plots the points and regression lines automatically, the 100-point rubric awards 0 points for graph plotting. Instead, Step 3 grades manual two-point tangent slope calculations, graph shape translations, and physical interval analysis.
  3. No "Auto-Populate Tangents" Shortcut: Students cannot click a button to have the computer fill in tangent slopes. On Graph 1 [$y(t)$], the simulation plots two distinct points $P_1(t_1, y_1)$ and $P_2(t_2, y_2)$ on the tangent line and hides the numerical slope until the student manually calculates and verifies at least 3 two-point tangent slopes using $v_y = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{t_2 - t_1}$.
  4. No Pre-Derived Textbook Shortcuts: In Step 4, students must show their full algebraic substitutions starting from fundamental kinematics equations ($y(t) = y_0 + v_{0y}t + \frac{1}{2}a_y t^2$, $v_y(t) = v_{0y} + a_y t$, $v_{fy}^2 = v_{0y}^2 + 2a_y\Delta y$).

3. Step-by-Step Teacher Walkthrough of the 5 Stages

Stage 1 & Stage 2

Video Calibration & Free-Flight Tracking

  • Teachers Available in Dropdown: Mr. Lopez, Ms. Chen, and Mr. Tipton.
  • Origin $(0,0)$: Students place the origin on the ground directly below the release point (or at the launch point).
  • Scale Stick: Students drag the orange bar across a known length in the video plane and click Apply.
  • Free Flight Only: Students step frame-by-frame (|< and >| or arrow keys) and track 10–15 points after release and before landing, then click Set $t = 0$ at 1st Pt.
Stage 3

6-Graph Studio, Two-Point Tangents & Intervals

  • 3 Manual Two-Point Slopes Required: Students use $P_1(t_1, y_1)$ and $P_2(t_2, y_2)$ on the tangent line to compute $v_y = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{t_2 - t_1}$ 3 times before quick-logging unlocks for the remaining 7 points (10 total).
  • Signed $v$-$t$ Area Shading: Graph 3 [$v_y(t)$] shades $+\Delta y_{\text{up}}$ (Teal) and $\Delta y_{\text{down}} < 0$ (Amber).
  • Part A & Part B Checks: Students verify their graph translations and physical intervals ($t_{\text{apex}}$, upward/downward intervals, and $a_y < 0$ on $[0, t_{\text{end}}]$).
Stage 4

Guided Calculations & Master Comparison Table

  • Section 1B: Verifies $a_y = \frac{\Delta v_y}{\Delta t}$, $\Delta y_{\text{up}}$, $\Delta y_{\text{down}}$, $\Delta y_{\text{total}}$, $y_{\text{max}} = y_0 + \Delta y_{\text{up}}$, and $y_{\text{final}} = y_0 + \Delta y_{\text{total}}$.
  • Sections 2–6: Students show algebraic derivations for $\Delta x$, $t_{\text{hang}}$, $y_{\text{max}}$, $v_0$, and $\theta$.
  • Percent Difference Formula: Uses $\text{\% Diff} = \frac{\text{Calculated} - \text{Measured}}{\text{Measured}} \times 100\%$.
Stage 5

11-Slide PowerPoint, 4-Page PDF & JSON Session

  • Slide 1 Screenshot: Automatically embeds the calibrated video frame showing all tracked points, the orange scale stick, and the $(0,0)$ origin axes.
  • Slide 8 (New): Summarizes the student's Graph Translation, Physical Intervals, and Signed-Area calculations.
  • Slide 9 & 10: Shows algebraic derivations and the Master Comparison Table.

4. Academic Integrity: How the Originality Token Works

Format: PK-SPORTS-XXXX-YYYY-ZZZZ (e.g., PK-SPORTS-7A3F-9C12-E62D)
  • How It Is Generated: Blocks 1 (XXXX) and 2 (YYYY) are 16-bit hexadecimal hashes computed from the student's name, teacher, calibrated scale factor, and every tracked $(t, x, y)$ coordinate. Block 3 (ZZZZ) is the mathematical XOR parity checksum: $\text{Block}_3 = \text{Block}_1 \oplus \text{Block}_2$.
  • What If a Student Makes Up a Token or Asks ChatGPT? Any made-up or manually altered token will fail the bitwise parity check ($\text{Block}_1 \oplus \text{Block}_2 \neq \text{Block}_3$). Click the 🔍 Verify button next to the token badge in Step 4 to paste any token and test its mathematical validity in 1 second.
  • Full Session Verification: Have students submit their .json lab file along with their PowerPoint or PDF. Inside the 🔍 Verify modal, click Upload & Inspect Student .json File to recompute the exact token from their raw coordinate data and verify 100% authenticity.
